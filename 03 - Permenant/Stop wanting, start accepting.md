# Stop wanting, start accepting

### start accepting people
- There are many kinds of people. Some are good, Some are bad
- We can't change behavior of people, even behavior towards us, So stop try to change them, and Just **Accept them**
### Accepting Transience 
- Stop worrying about future
- We realized that, Time is so fast, and Eventually Everything is dust
- The things matters most at the time, Is dust in future and Replace by other things too
- So stop worrying about future, Live in present
- Everything is dust and ash, then Nothing to worry about
### Stop misfortune 
- Stop wishing for things 
- Stop worrying about goals
- Here the point is, Stop wishing for better life, Be the better, #amorfati 
- **Not to pray for easier life, Pray for better you**
### Accept the change
- Everything is dust and ash, But That is not at starting, So what happened : **Change**
- Change is the nature thing, that benefit to whole
- The change we see is just the replacement of the old man. And That is good for nature, humans and for everything 
### Start accepting yourself 
- #the-backward-law, The more we try to find ourselves, the more we couldn't 
- #the-backward-law, accept your **insecurities**. The more we run from that, The more we get close to that
- Accept your weakness, Accept your strengths 
- Everybody has different weakness and strength 
- Stop trying to be someone
