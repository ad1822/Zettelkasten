# The savers paradox 

### Don't save for future, you don't know
We do everything for future, for future ourselves, But what if that didn't come...

What if things we did, meant anything for future

By saving and worrying about future, We are just creating a Ford, that we are prisoners in

#### Live your life at present, and don't worry about future
