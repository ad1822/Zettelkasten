# I am choosing

Thought just popup randomly, and everything became clear.
As a man, as a student, as a learner, as a leader, as a son and As an observer, I always had this thought and what if I am not this person, what I am trying to behave, What if behind me, I am a whole different person behind that. But
The Answer is: **I am choosing**. I am choosing me be quite, be wiser, be that and that... But I am not even trying to... 
So that's the best answer I could get..
