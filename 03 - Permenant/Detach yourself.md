# Detach yourself
By not attach to anything, This is the most Stoic thought, I've learned. Humans run behind values that is not created by himself, But by society. They called those things “Society Standards” like money, position, trophies, rank. By those things, People measure each other. Like If You are in a good position at your job, or you have to good money then you're good and vice versa. But At the end of the life, When Everything fall apart, we've know that these are just *Shitty* #values 
- Also detach yourself meant, attach to things which are actually good for yourself, By comparing to other people, get release by others, We don't get anything. Besides don't get fooled by *success* and *failure*, Be steadily at every level of time in life, That's what **Detach yourself** meant.

### Fight club's detachment vs. Stoicism's detachment
- What I've noticed in [[../08 - Bookshelf/Fight Club|Fight Club]] is this, *Tyler* is nothing by ourselves, which is detached to things, But by in negative way. He's driven by #nihilism, So, he doesn't try to find value in anything. 
- On the other hand, #stoic says that detach to yourself, In a good way. Don't run from your **responsibilities**, don't run from your **work**, instead stop try to find happiness and meaning in those shitty #values, find the *right* purpose and #values and run behind that. 

Tyler rejects values. A Stoic filters values.  
Tyler annihilates the self. A Stoic shapes the self.  
Tyler escapes meaning. A Stoic chooses meaning.
