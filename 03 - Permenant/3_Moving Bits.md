# 3_Moving Bits


- Moving one and zero from one end to the link to the next

- The physical layer consists of devices and means of transmitting bits across computer networks. 
- A bit is the smallest representation of data that a computer can understand
- Ones and Zeros are sent across those network cables through a process called **modulation**.
- Modulation is a way of varying the voltage of this charge moving across the cable.


### Twisted pair cable
- The most common type of cabling used for connecting computing devices- features pairs of copper wires that are Twisted together.

- A standard cat six cable has eight 8 wires consisting of four twisted pairs inside a single jacket.

- These cables allow for **duplex communication**.

- **Duplex communication** the concept that information can flow in both directions across the cable. 

- The way networking cables ensure that duplex communication is possible is by reserving one or two pairs for communicating in one direction. They then use the other one or two pairs for communicating in the other direction.
