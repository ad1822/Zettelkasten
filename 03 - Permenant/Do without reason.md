# Do without reason 
If we find reason to do things, we are bargaining ourselves. so, that's the external reason
When there is no reason, no hope to live and still we do, that's the reason. We are doing. I am also connecting this thought to my [[Survival is the way]] thought. 

update:
There are two things. 
#### External purpose and Internal purpose
People who do find reason in external world, that's wrong. 
If you really want to find a reason or purpose to do things, find internally. 
Question *why ?* you want to do things. then that's how you find your own purpose and walk on that.
