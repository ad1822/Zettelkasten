# Nothing is free will and we are always choosing

At the end of day, Everything we did, we will do, or we could do... Everything is an illusion that we believe, we could control that...
There's nothing like **free will**. We are living in illusion that we could control that, ourselves... But that's just an illusion. Yaa, that is good because **Illusion** give us a **hope** that we could control, and so we do. 
But At the end of day, still we are choosing **what to do and don't** and that is right...
