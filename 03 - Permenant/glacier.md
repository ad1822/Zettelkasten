# Glacier

- Data archiving service that is designed for security, durability and an extremely low cost.
- Designed to provide 11 9s of durability for objects 
- Supports encryption of data in transit and at rest through Secure Sockets Layer (SSL) or TLS
- Low cost data archiving and long term backup
- Configurable lifecycle archiving of S3 content to S3 Glacier
