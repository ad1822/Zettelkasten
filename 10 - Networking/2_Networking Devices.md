# Networking Devices

#### 1. Cables

- Connect different devices to each other, allowing data to be transmitted over them.

##### Two Types of Cables
1. Copper
- The most common forms of copper twisted-pair cables used in networking are Cat5, Cat5e, and Cat6 cables.
- Use electrical voltages.
2. Fiber
- Contain individual optical fibers, which are tiny tubes made out of glass about the width of a human hair.
- Use pulses of light to represent the ones and zeros of the underlying data

- Cables allowed you to point to point communication
- Single device at each end.

#### 2. Hub 

- Physical layer device that allows for connections from many computers at once.

![[hub.png]]

#### 3. Switch

- A switch is very similar to a hub since you can connect many devices to it so they can communicate.
- Hub is a Layer 1 or Physical layer device, a switch is a layer 2 or datalink layer device.
- This means that a switch can actually inspect the contents of the Ethernet
- protocol data being sent around the network, determine which system
- the data is intended for, and then only send that data to that one system.
