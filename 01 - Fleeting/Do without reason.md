# Do without reason 
If we find reason to do things, we are bargaining ourselves. 
Live without reason is the way to live.
When there is no reason, no hope to live and still we do, that's the reason. We are doing. I am also connecting this thought to my [[Survival is the way]] thought. 
If you are trying to be perfect, then there is no reason to live...
