# Happiness is a problem

---
### There is no value in suffering when it's done without purpose
People who pursue worldly pleasure suffer because of their wordly pleasure

___
### Pain and loss are inevitable, and we should let go of trying to resist them
---
### Happiness is not a solvable equation
---
We lose the benefits of experiencing healthy doses of pain, a loss that disconnects us from the reality of the world around us

---
### The solution to one problem is merely the creation of the next problem
Problems never go away, they just improved
When we solve a problem, there is big problem next, but thing is solving a problem is not about problem, It's about us

---
### Happiness comes from solving a problem, And that is life. Because problems are inevitable
True happiness occurs only when you find the problem you enjoy having and enjoy solving
