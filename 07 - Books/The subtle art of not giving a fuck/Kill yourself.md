# Kill yourself

### When we let go of the story we tell about ourselves, to ourselves, we free ourselves up to actually act (and fail) and grow.
Buddhism argues that your idea of who “you” are is an arbitrary mental construction and that you should let go o the idea that “you” exist at all; that the arbitrary metrics b which you define yourself actually trap you, and thus you're better off letting go of everything. In a sense, you could say that Buddhism encourages you to not give a fuck.

---
### Don't be special; don't be unique
### Be broader like an ocean
Redefine your metrics in mundane and broad ways. Choose to measure yourself not as a rising star or an undiscovered genius. Decide to measure yourself, not as some horrible victim or dismal failure. Instead, measure yourself by more mundane identities: a student, a partner, a friend, a creator.

The narrower and rarer the identity you decide for yourself, the more everything will seem to threaten you. For that reason, define yourself in the simplest and most ordinary ways possible.
