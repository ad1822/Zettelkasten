### Bravery vs. Heroism
Bravery is common, Resilience is common, But Heroism has a philosophical component to it.
There's some greatest **why** ?
We lost clear **why**?

---
### Why we care ?  (we are just distracting ourselves)
We care because, to need to feel that sense of importance in order to avoid the incomprehensibility of your existence, to avoid being crushed by the weight of your own material insignificance

Something needs to matter because without something mattering, then there's no reason to go on living. And some form of altruism or a reduction in suffering is always our mind's go-to for making it feel like it's worth doing anything.

Our psyches contract little narratives like this whenever they face adversity, these before/after stories we invent for ourselves. We keep those narratives alive to protecting our minds from the Uncomfortable truth.

---
### The paradox of Progress (today's culture)
#### The better things get, the more anxious and desperate we all seem to feel
#### The wealthier and after the place you live, the more likely you are to commit suicide

---
### Hope
Hope doesn't care about the problems that have already been solved, Hope cares about the problems that still need to be solved. Because **the better the world gets, the more we have to lose. and the more we have to lose, the less we feel we have to hope for.**

##### Three things
1. A sense of control
2. Values means we find something important enough to work toward
3. Community

--- 
### Everything being fucked doesn't require hope; hope requires everything being fucked
Hope requires something be broken. Hope requires that we renounce a part of ourselves and/ or a part of the world. It requires us to be anti-something.
